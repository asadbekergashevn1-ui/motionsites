## DESCRIPTION (the big text box — paste all of this)

You already know you're supposed to eat more protein. Nobody told you HOW —
when your stomach now holds about a cup, and half of that is gone before the
protein arrives.

This is not another meal plan. It is a planning system for one specific
problem: being physically unable to eat enough on a GLP-1 medication.

────────────────────────

WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

Most GLP-1 advice tells you to start with fibre and fill half your plate with
vegetables. That's good advice — for someone who isn't on the drug. On a GLP-1
it spends the little room you have and hands back two grams of protein.

This plan reverses the order. Protein first. Density before volume. It teaches
you one number no other guide uses: how much protein every 100 ml of your
stomach space has to carry — and which foods can actually do it.

────────────────────────

WHAT'S INSIDE — 40 PAGES

• Your two numbers — your protein target and your real capacity, worked out
  once in ten minutes, no maths required (read it off a table)
• The daily page — tick the line that sounds like today, eat from that row.
  Everyday portions, grams already counted
• Protein density tables — what each food gives you AND what it costs you in
  space, calculated from USDA FoodData Central
• Dose Week Mode — a plan for the week after every dose increase
• The texture ladder — four levels for four kinds of morning
• When fat is the problem — same protein, lighter cooking
• Nausea, constipation & diarrhoea — one page, three fixes
• Water, eating by the clock, and protecting your muscle
• Red flags — what needs a call today, with a cut-out fridge card
• Shopping list, first-week checklist, dose-week tracker, daily cards
• A sources page — every number traced, every own-design structure labelled

────────────────────────

THIS IS FOR YOU IF
