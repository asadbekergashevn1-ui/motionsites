## 4. FAQ

**Is this a meal plan?**
No. It is a planning system. It tells you what each food is worth in the space
you have, how to get protein in on a bad day, and what to do the week after a
dose increase. It does not give you seven days of recipes.

**I already track my protein in an app. Why do I need this?**
An app counts what you ate. It does not help you eat it. This works on the
problem before the counting: what fits, in what order, on a day when nothing
appeals.

**Are the numbers reliable?**
The protein figures are calculated from USDA FoodData Central, and the page
shows the method. Where something is our own planning structure rather than
published guidance — the dose-week percentages, the texture levels — the page
says so plainly rather than dressing it up as a protocol.

**Do I need a printer?**
No. The pages work on a phone or tablet, and the grocery list is designed to be
read in a shop. Printing helps for the pages you fill in by hand.

**A4 or US Letter?**
Both are included. Use whichever your printer takes.

**I'm not on a GLP-1 — will this help me?**
Probably not, and we would rather say so. This is built for people whose
appetite has been reduced by medication. If you are trying to raise your own
GLP-1 naturally, the advice here runs the other way.

**Can I get a refund?**
Digital files cannot be returned once downloaded. If something is unclear
before you buy, message us first — we would rather answer a question than take
a sale that disappoints.
