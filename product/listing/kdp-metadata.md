## DESCRIPTION  (paste into the Description box)

You already know you're supposed to eat more protein. Nobody told you HOW — when your stomach now holds about a cup, and half of that is gone before the protein arrives.

This is not another meal plan or recipe book. It is a 26-page planning system for one specific problem: being physically unable to eat enough protein on a GLP-1 medication.

WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

Most GLP-1 advice tells you to start with fiber and fill half your plate with vegetables. That's good advice — for someone who isn't on the medication. On a GLP-1 it spends the little room you have and gives back two grams of protein.

This plan reverses the order. Protein first. Density before volume. It teaches you one number no other guide uses: how much protein every 100 ml of your stomach space has to carry — and exactly which foods can do it.

WHAT'S INSIDE — 26 PAGES YOU USE, NOT READ

- Your two numbers: protein target and real capacity, worked out once, no math required
- The 30-second morning page: tick how today feels, eat from that row
- Protein density tables: what each food gives you AND what it costs you in space, from USDA FoodData Central
- Dose Week Mode: a plan for the week after every dose increase, before it arrives
- When fat is the problem: the same protein, cooked lighter
- Nausea, constipation and diarrhea: one page, three fixes
- Water, eating by the clock, and protecting your muscle
- Red flags: what's normal and what needs a call today, with a cut-out fridge card
- Shopping list, first-week checklist, dose-week tracker, daily cards
- A full sources page: every number traced and dated, every own-design structure labeled honestly

THIS IS FOR YOU IF

- You're on semaglutide or tirzepatide and struggling to eat enough
- You've been told to hit a protein target and can't see how
- The week after a dose increase undoes your progress every time
- You want a printed system you can write on, not another app

This is an educational planning tool, not medical advice. Protein figures come from USDA FoodData Central; the clinical framing follows the 2025 joint advisory of four professional societies. Where a structure is the author's own design rather than published guidance, the book says so. Always speak to your prescriber before changing how you eat, and never change your dose on your own. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the makers of Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro or Zepbound.
