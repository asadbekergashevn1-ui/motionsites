GLP-1 LOW APPETITE PROTEIN PLAN

You were given a
prescription.
Not a plan.

The appointment covered the dose and how to inject it. It did not cover what to eat on a morning when nothing appeals, how to hit a protein target in a stomach that now holds about a cup, or which signs mean you should pick up the phone today.

So you searched. At eleven at night, on your phone, you read five articles that all said the same four words: eat more protein.

None of them said how — when half your room is gone before the protein arrives, and the one thing you managed yesterday turns your stomach today.

This is not another hundred pages
of advice to get through.
It is twenty-six pages you use — most days, only one of them.
WHAT THIS IS NOT
✗A diet. Nothing here asks you to eat less.
✗Theory. There is no chapter on how the drug works.
✗Recipes you will never cook on a bad day.
✗“Listen to your body.” Your body has stopped asking.
✗Anything about your dose. That belongs to your prescriber.
WHAT IT IS
✓Two numbers that are yours — worked out in ten minutes, once.
✓One page for the morning. Tick a line. Eat from it.
✓A plan for dose week, before dose week arrives.
✓Food measured in space, not just in grams.
✓A line you do not cross alone — and what to say on the call.
10MINUTES
Plan it. Do it. Keep what you built.
Ten minutes today sets up the next month. After that it is thirty seconds a morning — and you stop looking things up at midnight.
