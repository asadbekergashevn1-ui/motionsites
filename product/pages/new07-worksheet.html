FOUR THINGS THAT TUNE THE NUMBER — WORTH RAISING WITH YOUR DOCTOR
You're over 60
Muscle holds on harder with protein at the upper half of the range plus the strength page — age makes both matter more, not less.
Losing fast (>1 kg a week)
Faster loss leans harder on lean mass — the upper half of the range earns its keep here too.
Any kidney history
This is the one where Path A is the only path — your clinician sets this number, full stop.
Weight is changing
Re-read the table each 10 kg of change — the target follows your weight down.
