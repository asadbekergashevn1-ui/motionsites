KEEPING IT RUNNING  ·  15WHEN HUNGER STOPS REMINDING YOU

Your appetite quit its job.
The clock takes over.

Hunger used to be your scheduler — it told you when, and roughly how much. The medicine has silenced it. Nothing replaces it by itself, which is how whole afternoons disappear and you reach evening at 20 grams. So you stop eating by feel and start eating by time.
Small and often beats big and rare:
four to six small meals — not two or three large ones you can no longer finish.

Set five phone alarms, once. Label them with the food, not “eat” — “eggs” at 10:30 removes the decision, which on this medicine is the hard part.
An alarm you can't face is information, not failure: drop that sitting one texture rung (page 12) instead of skipping it. Skipped sittings don't come back — the room doesn't carry over.
Why this works: clinical guidance for GLP-1 patients favours 4–6 small meals over 2–3 large ones — less pressure per sitting on a slowed stomach, steadier intake across the day. The times and foods above are an example to copy or shift, not a prescription.
