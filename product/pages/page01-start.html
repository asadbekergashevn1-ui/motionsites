2YOUR PROTEIN TARGET Your clinician's number always wins
Body weight KG  ×  1.2  =  G — LOWER TARGET
Body weight KG  ×  1.6  =  G — UPPER TARGET
1.2–1.6 g per kg is the range proposed for people actively reducing weight. If your clinician gave you a different number, use theirs and ignore this box. Staying under roughly 0.4–0.5 g per kg for a long stretch is where muscle loss starts — that is the floor this plan is built to keep you off.
3YOUR REAL CAPACITY Measure it for three days — don't guess
Comfortable in one sitting ML  ×  meals a day  =  ML PER DAY
Use a measuring cup for three ordinary days and write down the honest number, not the one you wish for. A cup is 240 ml. Most people on a GLP-1 land somewhere near 180 ml a sitting.
