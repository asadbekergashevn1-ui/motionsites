3
Protein intake consensus for adults on GLP-1 therapy
General GLP-1 patient guidance clusters at 1.0–1.5 g per kg per day, 20–30 g per sitting; the 2025 Advisory proposes 1.2–1.6 g/kg during active weight reduction — the range this guide's lookup table prints.
USED FOR: the target table on page 5 and every daily total in the examples
FOOD DATA
4
USDA FoodData Central — SR Legacy dataset
U.S. Department of Agriculture, fdc.nal.usda.gov, accessed November 2026. Protein per 100 g converted to protein per 100 ml using USDA household-measure cup weights for each food. Four values (turkey, prawns, cod, salmon) are derived from the measured packing density of comparable foods rather than direct cup weights, and are marked as estimates in our records.
USED FOR: every g/100 ml figure on pages 7–10 and the grocery list on page 20
HOW THIS PLAN WAS BUILT — THE STRUCTURES THAT MAKE IT WORK
→The dose-week dial (60–80–90–100%): the pattern — symptoms cluster after dose steps and ease within days to weeks — is from the Advisory. The specific percentages are our planning scaffold so a hard week has numbers instead of guilt.
→The texture ladder (L1–L4): our organisation of commonly tolerated food textures into rungs with reachable targets. It is not a dysphagia or medical texture scale.
→The capacity measurement and target-÷-capacity arithmetic: a simple method built on a plain fact — a GLP-1 stomach holds less per sitting. You measure your own capacity and re-measure it after dose changes, so the plan always fits the stomach you have today.
This is an educational planning guide built from published sources and careful arithmetic. It is not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before starting this plan, and follow their guidance and the information that came with your medicine.
