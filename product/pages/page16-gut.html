KEEPING IT RUNNING  ·  22ONE SLOWED STOMACH, THREE COMPLAINTS

Constipation. Nausea. Diarrhoea.
Same cause — three different fixes.

All three trace back to the same thing: the medicine changed the speed of your gut. Which complaint you get decides what to do — find your column, do the top thing first.
Constipation
Everything is moving slower — and you're eating less fibre and drinking less than you used to, without noticing.
DO FIRST
1Water before fibre. Fibre without fluid sets like cement. 8–10 glasses across the day.
2Add fibre gradually — oats, kiwi, berries, a spoon of ground flax in yogurt. Sudden fibre = bloating.
3Walk 20–30 minutes daily. Movement outside moves things inside.
GO EASY ON
✗Big raw-vegetable piles — fibre, yes, but they also spend your protein room.
Call today if: no movement for several days with pain, swelling, or vomiting.
Nausea
A full-feeling stomach reporting in — loudest in your first weeks and the days after each dose rise.
DO FIRST
1Go smaller, not never. Half portions, twice as often. An overfull sitting echoes for hours.
2Go cold and bland. Cold chicken, yogurt, boiled eggs — smell is half the trigger and cold food barely has one.
3Ginger or peppermint tea, small sips. Old remedies, decent evidence, zero downside.
GO EASY ON
✗Fried, greasy, rich — the fat page exists for exactly these days.
Call today if: you can't keep fluids down for 24 hours, or vomiting is repeated.
Diarrhoea
The other direction — for some people the gut answers the medicine by emptying fast, especially early on.
DO FIRST
1Replace what's leaving: water plus an electrolyte sachet or salty broth — not water alone.
2Soluble fibre firms things up — oats, banana, apple without the skin. It absorbs water on the way through.
3Keep protein gentle: eggs, yogurt, poached chicken — the target still stands on these days.
GO EASY ON
✗Greasy, spicy, and sugar-free sweeteners ending in “-ol” — all three speed things up.
Call today if: it lasts beyond a couple of days, or with blood, fever, or dizziness.
⇄
They can alternate — that's normal with this medicine. Constipated on dose week, loose the week after. Treat whichever column is true today, keep fluids running through both, and log it on the dose-week tracker — your prescriber will want the pattern, not the adjectives.
