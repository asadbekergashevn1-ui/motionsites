SETUP  ·  04NINETY SECONDS THAT COME BEFORE EVERYTHING

Before anything else:
eight honest ticks.

This plan is built for a typical adult on GLP-1 therapy. The eight situations below change what is safe or sensible — not because you're fragile, but because the right plan for them is a different plan, and pretending otherwise is how guides earn one-star reviews and you earn a bad month.
TICK ANYTHING THAT APPLIES TO YOU, TODAYBE HONEST — NOBODY'S WATCHING
Pregnant, trying, or breastfeeding
protein maths for two is clinician territory, full stop
Type 1 diabetes
meal timing and carbohydrate rules override everything in here
Kidney disease — or a history of kidney stones
“eat more protein” is exactly the advice that needs a doctor's sign-off first
History of pancreatitis or gallbladder disease
these interact with the medicine itself — your prescriber may already be watching
Any history of an eating disorder
targets and tracking can be the wrong tools entirely — please take this one seriously
Mood getting darker, or any thoughts of self-harm
reported on these medicines; it outranks every food question on every page
Can't keep fluids down for 24 hours or more
that's not a planning problem — that's today's phone call
No bowel movement for 3+ days, with pain or swelling
same — the gut page can wait, the call can't
■
Ticked anything? Stop here. Not forever — just until a clinician who knows your history has looked at it. Bring this guide along if you like; the right version of this plan for you starts with their adjustments, not with page 5.
WHAT THIS GUIDE HANDLES
What to eat, in what order, in how much space. When a hard day is normal and how to eat through it. What to write down so appointments run on data.
WHAT IT NEVER TOUCHES
Your dose. Your injections. Your other medicines. Diagnosis of anything. Those belong to your prescriber — no page here second-guesses them.
