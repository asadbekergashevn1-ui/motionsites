THE FOOD PAGES  ·  14NONE OF THESE ARE BAD FOODS. THEY ARE THE WRONG TOOL.

Foods that spend your space.

Everything below is “healthy.” That is exactly the trap — healthy advice is written for people with room. You have about a cup. Here is what each one pays back for the room it takes.
Brown riceA cup fills a third of your capacity and pays back 5 g. Three bites, not a base.2.3
BroccoliFine food, wrong job. The same 18 g chicken gives in 100 ml costs five cups here.1.5
Avocado“Healthy fats” — and fat slows a stomach the medicine has already slowed.1.2
Salad leavesA big bowl of fullness, 1 g of protein. Eat it after the anchor if you still have room.1
Juice & smoothies (fruit)Liquid fills you too. A glass of juice is 250 ml of your day gone for ~1 g.<1
Brothy soup, plainComforting, near-zero payback — unless you fortify it (the grocery page shows how).<1
Whole fruit, melon, appleVitamins yes, protein no — half a cup after the anchor, as dessert.<1
Bread, crackers, cerealDry bulk that swells. The classic “I meant to eat the chicken next” mistake.~2
THE USUAL PLATE, IN YOUR STOMACH
Salad first, rice under it, chicken on top. You stop at half the plate — the half that was leaves and rice.
Room spent: 240 ml  ·  Protein: ~9 g
SAME FOODS, REVERSED
Chicken first, cottage cheese after, then rice and leaves if room remains. You stop at the same point.
Room spent: 240 ml  ·  Protein: ~33 g
↓
Nothing here is forbidden. The rule is only about order: these foods eat last, from whatever room the anchors leave behind. On a good day that is plenty. On a dose-week day it is nothing — and that is correct.
Why “fibre first” advice doesn't apply to you: standard guidance — vegetables first, half the plate green, fibre to slow digestion — is written to make a normal stomach feel full sooner. Your medicine already did that. Following both at once is how people end up at 30 g a day. The 2025 joint clinical Advisory notes fatty and high-fibre foods are the common triggers in the first days after dose changes — the dose-week page works on exactly this.
