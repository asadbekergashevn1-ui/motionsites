Red Flags

Stop reading the plan. Make the call.
Most of what this medication does to you is uncomfortable and ordinary. A few things are not. This page exists so you can tell them apart in ten seconds, without searching the internet at two in the morning.
GET HELP NOWEMERGENCY CARE
Severe stomach pain that spreads through to your back, with or without vomiting.
Sharp pain under the right ribs together with fever, chills, or yellowing of the skin or eyes.
Vomiting you cannot stop, with dizziness, confusion, or a racing heart.
CALL YOUR CLINICIAN TODAYDON'T WAIT FOR THE NEXT APPOINTMENT
Nothing stays down for 24 hours or more.
Much less urine than usual, or very dark urine.
No bowel movement for 3 days, with discomfort.
Pain in the upper right belly after meals.
Mood sliding, or any thought of harming yourself.
Side effects still severe after the first few weeks.
EXPECTED — NOT A REASON TO PANICTHIS IS THE MEDICATION WORKING
Feeling full after a few bites.
Mild nausea for a week or two after a dose step.
Burping, or mild reflux.
Much less interest in food than before.
Constipation that settles with fluids and fibre.
Food you used to love suddenly not appealing.
One thing is never optional: fluids. Vomiting or diarrhoea can dry you out quickly enough to injure your kidneys — and that can happen whether or not you have ever had kidney trouble. If you cannot drink, that is itself a reason to ring someone, even when nothing else on this page applies.

THEY WILL PROBABLY ASK
· How much are you drinking, and can you keep it down?
· How much urine, and what colour?
· Any pain — where exactly, and does it move?
· Any fever?
· How much weight in how many weeks?
Having the answers ready is the difference between a five-minute call and a wasted one.
