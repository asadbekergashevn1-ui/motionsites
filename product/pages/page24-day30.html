IF THE NUMBERS MOSTLY HELD
The system fits you. Keep the morning page and the trackers running, re-measure capacity after each dose change, and let the rest of this guide go back on the shelf until a hard week pulls it down.
IF THEY MOSTLY DIDN'T
The sheets now show you exactly where. Low every morning → page 15's clock. Low on dose weeks only → that is what the dial predicted — stock the shelf earlier. Low everywhere and falling → that is a prescriber conversation, and you're bringing the evidence.
