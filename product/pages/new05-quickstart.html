Quick start. Three moves, then breakfast.

Everything else in this book is backup for hard days. The system itself is just this page — do the three moves once, and from tomorrow the whole plan is thirty seconds a morning.

One genuinely useful tip before you start: talk this plan through with your doctor if you can — not because you need permission, but because they know your exact situation better than any book, and a two-minute conversation can tune these numbers to you. If you have a number from them already, it wins.
