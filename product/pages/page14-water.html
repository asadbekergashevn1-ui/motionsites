KEEPING IT RUNNING  ·  14THE FORGOTTEN HALF OF LOW INTAKE

You're not just eating less.
You're drinking less — without noticing.

A surprising share of daily fluid normally arrives inside food — soups, fruit, sauces, the water bound up in every meal. Eat half as much and that supply quietly halves too, while the medicine blunts the signals that would have told you.
WHY IT MATTERS HERE
Dehydration makes nausea worse, makes constipation worse, and reads as tiredness and headache — all things you might blame on the medicine.
WHY YOU MISS IT
Thirst cues arrive late and quiet when intake drops. Waiting to feel thirsty stops working as a strategy — so the clock takes over, same as with food.
THE TIMING TRICK
Big drinks with meals spend stomach room you need for protein. Most fluid goes between meals — small and steady beats large and rare.

✓Cold and fizzy often land easier on a queasy stomach than warm and flat. Ice counts. Sparkling water counts.
✓Milk pulls double duty — a glass is 250 ml of fluid and 9 g of protein. The only drink on both pages.
✓On a bad day, add electrolytes — a sachet in water replaces what vomiting, diarrhoea or barely eating washes out.
✓Watch the colour. Pale straw: fine. Dark and scant: you are behind — catch up in sips, not pints.
WHEN THIS PAGE IS NOT ENOUGH
Can't keep fluids down for 24 hours, dizziness on standing, barely passing urine, or a dry mouth that water doesn't fix — that is dehydration outrunning what sipping can repair. Go to the Red Flags page and make the call today.
