THE FOOD PAGES  ·  15THE ORDER IS THE RULE. NOTHING IS BANNED.

Protein first — not protein only.

A body running on less food needs three more things beside protein, and they're easy to miss when portions shrink. You don't have to think hard about this — each one has a two-second trick that rides along with meals you're already eating.
Fibre
keeps everything moving — the gut page's best friend
Smaller meals quietly mean less fibre — and that's the fast lane to constipation. Aim toward 25 g a day (women) / 38 g (men), raised gradually.
THE TWO-SECOND TRICKS
+Spoon of ground flax into any yogurt
+Berries on breakfast — already in the good day
+Oats or kiwi on constipated mornings
Dose week? Ease off for 2–3 days, then build back — the dial page covers it.
Three quiet minerals
calcium · iron · vitamin D — absorbed less when meals shrink
Nothing exotic needed — just pairings. The right combinations do the work your smaller portions can't.
THE PAIRING RULES
+Iron + vitamin C: lentils or beef with peppers or an orange
+Vitamin D + a little fat: salmon and eggs carry their own
×Iron apart from coffee & tea — they block it. One hour's gap.
Dairy anchors (yogurt, cottage cheese) quietly cover calcium already. ✓
Enough fuel
protein is the priority — not the whole menu
The anchor eats first. But rice, fruit, bread and olive oil belong on the plate after it — energy is what keeps you walking, working and training.
HOW IT LOOKS IN REAL LIFE
+The good day already does this: rice and veg after chicken
+Fruit as dessert — after, never instead
+Running on empty for days → that's a clinician chat, not more discipline
One sentence to remember: anchors first, colours after, nothing banned.
