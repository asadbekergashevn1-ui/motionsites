The one rule this page asks of you: protein first, always. Whatever is on the plate, the anchor goes in before anything else touches your room. If you stop early — and some days you will — you stopped after the part that mattered.
Where these numbers come from: protein per 100 g from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy), converted to per-100 ml using USDA cup weights for each food. Four values (turkey, prawns, cod, salmon) are derived from measured packing density rather than direct cup weights — marked in the method notes.
