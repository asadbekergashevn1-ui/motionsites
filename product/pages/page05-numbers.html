Between two columns? Take the lower one to start with. Starting low and climbing beats missing every day.
1.2–1.6 g per kg is the range proposed for people actively reducing weight. If your clinician gave you a different number, use theirs and ignore this box. Staying under roughly 0.4–0.5 g per kg for a long stretch is where muscle loss starts — that is the floor this plan is built to keep you off.
2YOUR REAL CAPACITY Measure it for three days — don't guess
Comfortable in one sitting ML  ×  meals a day  =  ML PER DAY
Use a measuring cup for three ordinary days and write down the honest number, not the one you wish for. A cup is 240 ml. There is no normal figure to aim for here — capacity varies a lot between people and changes with every dose step, which is exactly why you measure yours.

Worked example. Maria is 82 kg, so her target is 98 g. She measured 180 ml a sitting across four meals, which is 720 ml of space a day. 98 ÷ 7.2 = 13.6 g per 100 ml — the amber band.
What that means for her: almost every 100 ml has to be green-tier — chicken, tofu, tuna, cottage cheese — and one concentrated shake closes the last stretch. A plate of salad and brown rice would quietly cost her the whole day.
That last figure is not a judgement. It is the size of the job — and the next pages tell you which foods can do it.
