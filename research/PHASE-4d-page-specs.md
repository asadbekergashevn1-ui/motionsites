## BET 1 — START HERE
**Sarlavha:** START HERE · **Subtitle:** "Two minutes. Then you'll know your number."

**Blok A — SAFETY FIRST (qizil ramka, tepada):**
6 ta belgilash katagi:
- Pregnant or breastfeeding
- Type 1 diabetes
- History of pancreatitis or gallbladder disease
- **History of kidney stones** (hayvon proteini ogohlantirishi)
- History of an eating disorder
- Can't keep fluids down for 24+ hours

→ Agar birortasi belgilansa: **"Stop. Talk to your clinician before using this plan."**

**Blok B — YOUR NUMBER:**
Oddiy hisob qutisi:
```
Your weight (kg) ____ × 1.2 = ____ g   (lower target)
Your weight (kg) ____ × 1.6 = ____ g   (upper target)
```
Izoh (kichik): *"These are the targets proposed for active weight reduction.
**If your clinician gave you a different number, use theirs.**"*
⚠️ Pastki chegara ogohlantirishi: *"Below 0.4–0.5 g/kg for a long period risks muscle loss."*

**Blok C — YOUR CAPACITY:**
```
How much can you comfortably eat in one sitting?  ____ ml
How many times a day do you eat?                  ____
```
→ `Capacity × meals = your real daily volume`

**Blok D — THE GAP (amber quti):**
```
TARGET ____ g  −  WHAT SOLIDS GIVE YOU ____ g  =  GAP ____ g
```
*"That gap is what liquids and concentrated sources have to cover. This is arithmetic, not willpower."*

## BET 3 — DOSE WEEK MODE ⭐⭐ (eng kuchli differensiator)
**Sarlavha:** DOSE WEEK MODE · **Subtitle:** "The week after a dose increase is predictable. Plan it."

**Tepada — mantiq (1 qator):** *"Side effects track dose steps and usually ease within 1–2 weeks. That makes this schedulable."*

**Asosiy vizual — 7 kunlik gorizontal tasma:**

| Kun | Tekstura | Protein maqsadi | Diqqat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | L1–L2 | **×0.6** | Suyuqlik ustuvor |
| 3–4 | L2 | **×0.8** | Kichik, tez-tez |
| 5–6 | L3 | **×0.9** | Qattiqqa qaytish |
| 7 | L4 | **×1.0** | Normal |

⚠️ **Muhim qoida qutisi:** *"Target drops — it never goes to zero."*

**Yon quti — WHAT CHANGES THIS WEEK:**
- ↓ Yog'li ovqat (yog' bo'shashni yana sekinlashtiradi)
- ↓ **Tola** (dastlabki kunlarda kamaytiriladi, keyin qaytariladi)
- ↑ Suyuqlik (ovqat **orasida**, ovqat paytida emas)
- ✗ Spirtli ichimlik
- ✗ Katta porsiya (qusish aynan shundan)

**Pastda:** *"Ask your clinician about a slower titration. Never change your dose on your own."*

## BET 4 — TEXTURE LADDER ⭐
**Sarlavha:** TEXTURE LADDER · **Subtitle:** "Match the food to today, not to the plan."

**Vizual: 4 pog'onali narvon, pastdan yuqoriga, ikki tomonlama strelka bilan**

| Daraja | Qachon | Misollar |
|---|---|---|
| **L4** Regular | Yaxshi kun | Tovuq, baliq, tuxum, tvorog |
| **L3** Soft & moist | Yengil belgilar | Qiyma, baliq, omlet, yunon yogurti |
| **L2** Puree / thick liquid | O'rtacha belgilar | Sho'rva-pyure, qalin shake, tvorog |
| **L1** Clear, cold, thin | Eng og'ir kun | Suyultirilgan shake, sovuq tiniq protein |

**Yon izoh (fiziologiya):** *"Thin, cold, watery foods leave the stomach faster. That's why L1 works on the worst days."*

⚠️ **Asosiy farq qutisi:** *"Unlike surgery recovery, this ladder goes **both ways**. You step down on a dose week and climb back after."*

## BET 5 — RED FLAGS
**Sarlavha:** RED FLAGS · **Subtitle:** "Stop reading the plan. Make the call."

**🚨 EMERGENCY (qizil blok):**
- Severe stomach pain that spreads to your back
- Sharp pain under the right ribs + yellow skin/eyes, or fever and chills

**📞 CALL YOUR CLINICIAN (amber blok):**
- Can't keep food or fluids down for 24+ hours
- No bowel movement for 3+ days with discomfort
- Passing much less urine than usual
- Right upper belly pain after meals
- Mood getting worse, or thoughts of self-harm
- Side effects still severe after the first few weeks

**Pastda (navy):** *"Dehydration from vomiting or diarrhoea can injure your kidneys. Fluids are not optional."*
